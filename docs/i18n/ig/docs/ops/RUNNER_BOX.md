Ìgwè a na-akwado n'onwe ya na-agba n'igwe **.113** (`tomni-proxmox-113`). Kemgbe 2026-10-18, runner
OmniRoute ọ bụla nwere **otu ọrụ**, otu akara, na oke ebe nchekwa/CPU a haziri dịka nha ibu ọrụ ya
gosiri. Oghere Turbopack na-enweta ihe build ahụ chọrọ; a haziri oghere ndị ọzọ dịka
ọnụ ọgụgụ kachasị elu e tụrụ ha. Igwe ahụ na-ekwe nkwa akụrụngwa karịrị nke o nwere naanị mgbe build Turbopack dakọrọ na build webpack
Release-Green na ọrụ lint; swap nke host (16 GB) na-anabata nke ahụ, ma n'ihi na Builds nke pull-request na-agba
na sistemụ hosted, oghere Turbopack anaghị arụ ọrụ ọtụtụ oge:

| runner               | akara          | ọrụ                                                                                                                                                 | `MemoryHigh` / `MemoryMax` | `CPUQuota`       |
| -------------------- | -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- | ---------------- |
| `omniroute-113-5`    | `omni-build`   | Builds Turbopack: `ci.yml` `Build` (naanị push/dispatch), Docker amd64, `npm-publish`, `nightly-v4-build`, `preview-artifact`, build a11y nke abalị | 56 GB / 60 GB (swap 0)     | 1600 %           |
| `omniroute-113-6`    | `omni-release` | Release-Green (`release-green` + `main-green`), build webpack                                                                                       | 20 GB / 24 GB (swap 4 GB)  | 800 %            |
| `omniroute-113-2`    | `omni-light`   | ọrụ abalị (Schemathesis, promptfoo, garak, k6, mbido a11y) na `quality.yml` `lint-guard`                                                            | 12 GB / 16 GB (swap 4 GB)  | 600 %            |
| `omniroute-113`      | `omni-light`   | otu ìgwè ahụ dị n'elu                                                                                                                               | 8 GB / 12 GB               | 400 %            |
| OmniHeuris, OmniMind | (repos ha)     | Naanị Dependabot (ọnụ ọgụgụ kachasị elu 155 MB nke ọ bụla, 2026-10-18)                                                                              | 4 GB / 6 GB                | 200 % nke ọ bụla |

A tụrụ na 2026-10-18:

| akụrụngwa | uru                        | ihe ọ pụtara maka nhazi oge                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --------- | -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| RAM / CPU | **78 GB / cores 32**       | build production Turbopack ruru 49 GB mgbe enweghị oke (`tests/unit/ci-build-bundler-webpack.test.ts`); na Next 16.3.8, naanị usoro compile na-ejide ihe dịka 40 GB (36 GB RSS n'ime `next-build` tinyere ndị ọrụ loader 17 na 3 GB, dịka e tụrụ na 2026-10-18); build webpack na-eji ihe dịka 14–16 GB. Otu build Turbopack + otu build webpack nwere ike ịbanye ọnụ; builds Turbopack abụọ enweghị ike |
| swap      | 16 GB                      | `omniroute-113-5` na-agba ebe agbanyụrụ swap (`MemorySwapMax=0`) ka e wee kwụsị build na-apụ n'oke n'ókè ya kama ime ka igwe ahụ jiri swap                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| `/tmp`    | **12 GB tmpfs = RAM**      | ihe ọ bụla edobere ebe ahụ bụ ebe nchekwa; a na-ekpochapụ ihe ndị fọdụrụ mgbe 3 h gachara                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| CPU       | a hụrụ load 60 na cores 32 | tupu e tinye oke ndị ahụ, otu build Turbopack (~1166 % CPU) tinyere suite coverage n'otu igwe ahụ mere ka heartbeats nke listeners ndị ọzọ ghara inweta akụrụngwa zuru ezu (`The self-hosted runner lost communication`, #16853)                                                                                                                                                                         |
| runners   | **listeners 6**            | OmniRoute 4 + OmniHeuris + OmniMind; agbanyụrụ `omniroute-113-3/-4/-7/-8` (`systemctl enable --now` na-eweghachi otu n'ọrụ)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |

Oke ndị ahụ dị na `/etc/systemd/system/actions.runner.<…>.service.d/override.conf` nke unit ọ bụla
(e debere uru ndị gara aga dịka `override.conf.bak-20261018`) ma tinye ha ozugbo site na
`systemctl set-property --runtime … MemoryHigh=… MemoryMax=… CPUQuota=…` — enweghị restart, ya mere ọ dịghị
ọrụ na-agba agba e metụrụ. A na-agbanwe akara site na runners API
(`gh api -X DELETE repos/<repo>/actions/runners/<id>/labels/<label>` /
`gh api -X POST repos/<repo>/actions/runners/<id>/labels -f 'labels[]=<label>'`), ozugbo kwa.

## Wụnye onye nhicha (naanị otu ugboro, n'igwe ahụ)

```bash
scp scripts/ops/runner-janitor.sh root@192.168.0.113:/opt/omniroute-ops/runner-janitor.sh
ssh root@192.168.0.113 'chmod +x /opt/omniroute-ops/runner-janitor.sh; apt-get install -y lsof'
# cron (root): kwa nkeji 30, dekọọ ndekọ na /var/log/runner-janitor.log
*/30 * * * * MAX_ACTIVE_RUNNERS=6 /opt/omniroute-ops/runner-janitor.sh >> /var/log/runner-janitor.log 2>&1
```

A chọrọ `lsof`: onye nhicha ahụ na-eji otu nseta nke faịlụ ndị mepere emepe
gosi na path anaghị arụ ọrụ tupu o wepụ ya; ma ọ bụrụ na enweghị ngwa ahụ, ọ naghị ewepụ
ihe ọ bụla, ọ na-ekwukwa nke ahụ (exit 1). Buru ụzọ jiri `--dry-run` nwalee mgbanwe ọ bụla —
ọ na-ebipụta kpọmkwem ihe ọ ga-eme ma ọ naghị emetụ ihe ọ bụla aka.

Ihe ọ na-eme na run ọ bụla: ọ na-ekpochapụ ihe fọdụrụ nke anyị (`runner-*`, `omniroute-*`,
`next-build*`, `e2e-build.tar.gz`) mgbe **awa 3 gasịrị na tmpfs** na awa 24 gasịrị na
`_work/_temp` dị na diski; ọ na-akwụsị `next-build` karịrị nkeji 75 (ọ dịghị job na-arụ ọrụ
ruo ogologo oge ahụ — na 2026-08-27, otu rụrụ nkeji 70 mgbe GitHub kwupụtachara na job ya
efuola); ọ na-ehichapụ checkouts ndị dị awa 48 nke runner ndị yuniti ha
**kwụsịrị**; ọ na-enye ọkwa mgbe ojiji diski ≥ 85 %, memory PSI `full/avg60` ≥ 10 %,
na mgbe ọnụ ọgụgụ listeners karịrị `MAX_ACTIVE_RUNNERS` (yana nkewa omniroute/other).
Exit 1 = a chọrọ nlebara anya; gụọ ndekọ ahụ.

Iwu nkeji 75 ahụ anaghị achọpụta build nke GitHub kwụsịrị ichere job ya. Mgbe
`Runner.Listener` enweghị ike imegharị job ya, `_diag/Runner_*.log` na-egosi
`POST request to …/renewjob timed out after 100 seconds`, API na-akọ na job ahụ bụ `completed`
(failure) ihe dị ka nkeji iri mgbe mmegharị ikpeazụ gara nke ọma, `next-build` na-aga n'ihu
na-arụ ọrụ n'enweghị job ejikọrọ na ya (2026-10-18: Build nke #17066 dara na 19:43 ma build ya
ka na-ejide 40 GB nkeji ise ka e mesịrị). Kwenye ref ahụ na `_diag/Worker_*.log` kachasị ọhụrụ,
wee jiri PID zipụ SIGTERM naanị na build tree — `next-build`, `npm run build`,
`build-next-isolated.mjs` na workers ndị dị na `_work`. Emetụla `Runner.Listener`,
`RunnerService.js` ma ọ bụ `Runner.Worker` aka mgbe ọ bụla: worker ahụ na-akọ na a kagbuola
job ahụ, listener ahụ na-alaghachikwa online n'onwe ya.

## Yuniti runner: KillMode

`KillMode=process` ndabara nke runner na-ahapụ `Runner.Worker → npm → next-build`
ka ha na-arụ ọrụ mgbe a kwụsịrị ma ọ bụ malitegharịa yuniti — build ghọrọ orphan na-aga
n'ihu iri RAM na CPU n'enweghị job ejikọrọ na ya. Yuniti OmniRoute ọ bụla nwere drop-in
(`/etc/systemd/system/actions.runner.diegosouzapw-OmniRoute.<name>.service.d/10-killmode.conf`)
nke nwere `KillMode=mixed`: SIGTERM na listener na mbụ, SIGKILL na cgroup dum mgbe
`TimeoutStop` ruru. Ọ ga-amalite ịrụ ọrụ na restart ọzọ nke yuniti ahụ — malitegharịa
**naanị otu runner n'otu oge, naanị mgbe ọ naghị arụ ọrụ**, tinyekwa idle check na restart
n'otu command ahụ.

## Iwu ọrụ

- **Naanị otu build Turbopack n’otu oge — a na-amanye nke a site na label.** Ọrụ ọ bụla na-eme build Turbopack zuru ezu
  `next build` na-ezubere `[self-hosted, omni-build]`, ma ọ bụ naanị **`omniroute-113-5`** nwere
  label ahụ: `ci.yml` `Build`, `npm-publish.yml` `publish`, `nightly-v4-build`, `preview-artifact`,
  build a11y abalị (#16853) na `docker-publish.yml` **amd64** (webpack n’ime BuildKit —
  Turbopack dara ebe ahụ, run 33253576569). Akụkụ Docker arm64 ka na-arụ na `ubuntu-24.04-arm`.
  amd64 nke `docker-publish` na `ci.yml` `Build` na-eji otu concurrency group `heavy-build-main`
  (`cancel-in-progress: false`). Docker Engine ga-adịrịrị na `omniroute-113-5`. Oghere Turbopack
  nke abụọ agaghị aba na 78 GB (2 × 33–37 GB tinyere ihe ndị ọzọ dị na igwe ahụ); oghere nke abụọ dị nchebe naanị maka
  build webpack.
- **Build pull request na-arụ na hosted runner (2026-10-18).** `ci.yml` `Build` na-erute `omni-build` naanị maka
  `push` na `workflow_dispatch`; `pull_request` ọ bụla (sitere n’otu origin ahụ ma ọ bụ fork) na Build `merge_group`
  na-arụ na `ubuntu-latest` site na webpack. Mgbe e zigara PR niile sitere n’otu origin ahụ n’oghere ahụ, otu Build Turbopack
  jidere ya nkeji 29–58, Build 18 nọ n’ahịrị ruo awa 18, Build push nke release line n’onwe ya
  nwetakwara oghere ahụ naanị otu ugboro n’ime ọrụ 40. Na hosted runner, otu Build PR ahụ na-ewe nkeji 8–12 n’akụkụ
  ọrụ ndị ọzọ nke PR ahụ. A ka na-anwale Turbopack, bundler nke `npm-publish` na-ebupu, na release line site na
  build a11y abalị nke default branch (04:41 UTC), site na Build push ma ọ bụ dispatch ọ bụla
  ruru oghere ahụ, nakwa site na `npm-publish` n’onwe ya. `tests/unit/build/heavy-build-queue.test.ts`
  na-eme ka gate nwere nkebi anọ ahụ guzosie ike.
- **Ebelatala nha oghere Turbopack ka ọ daa n’okpuru mkpa a tụrụ.** Na 2026-10-18 n’elekere 18:06, e wedatara oghere ahụ
  site na 56G/60G ruo 40G/44G iji nye `omni-release` ohere. Compile phase nọrọ na
  `MemoryHigh` n’oge run ya niile (memory PSI `full` 30–43 %), ma `Runner.Listener`, nke
  na-ekekọrịta cgroup ahụ, enweghị ike imegharị job ya. E weghachiri nhazi 56G/60G nke onye nwe ya sitere na 2026-10-17
  n’otu ụbọchị ahụ. N’okpuru uru ahụ, build ahụ anaghị ada; ọ na-agbalịsi ike ugboro ugboro, e mesịa job ahụ efuo.
- **Release-Green nwere oghere nke ya (2026-10-18).** Ruo mgbe ahụ, validation Release-Green abụọ ahụ na-arụ
  na `omni-build`: na push ọ bụla (~10/hour, a kagburu push run 97 n’ime 100 ka ha na-arụ ọrụ n’otu ụbọchị)
  na full sweep atọ nke na-ewe ihe dị ka awa 2 kwa ụbọchị (sweep nke 05:29 chere awa 6 maka oghere ahụ) — e nyere otu oghere
  build ahụ ọrụ karịrị ike ya ọtụtụ ugboro, Build PR sitere n’otu origin ahụ cherekwa ọtụtụ awa.
  `release-green` na `main-green` na-ezubere `omni-release` ugbu a (`omniroute-113-6` naanị) site na
  `OMNIROUTE_USE_TURBOPACK=0`, a naghịkwa akagbu run maliterela n’ihi push ọhụrụ (oghere
  pending ka na-achịkọta nnukwu mmụba ahụ). `tests/unit/build/runner-capacity-routing.test.ts`
  na-eme ka routing ahụ, mgbanwe webpack ahụ, na eziokwu na ọ dịghị ihe na `omni-release` ji Turbopack eme build guzosie ike.
- **Pool dị mfe: `omni-light`.** `omniroute-113` na `omniroute-113-2` na-arụ ọrụ abalị ndị chọrọ
  `next build` nke backend naanị (~5–6 GB; webpack) na `quality.yml` `lint-guard`. `omniroute-113`
  nwere `MemoryHigh=4G` ruo 2026-10-18 — build promptfoo jiri nkeji 54 na-agbalị iweghachite memory
  n’ebe ahụ; 8 GB na-ewepụ throttling ahụ.
- **E ji ụma belata ọnụ ọgụgụ listener.** A gbanyụrụ unit OmniRoute anọ na 2026-08-29 — listener ọ bụla agbakwunyere
  bụ tenant ọzọ maka memory budget dị n’elu. Oke janitor bụ 6 (`MAX_ACTIVE_RUNNERS=6` na cron): ọ na-agụ
  `Runner.Listener` ọ bụla dị na igwe ahụ, OmniHeuris + OmniMind na-agbakwunyekwa abụọ na anọ nke anyị.
- **Ejila aka hichapụ `/tmp` ma ọ bụ `_work` mgbe runner ọ bụla ka na-arụ ọrụ.** Usoro
  check-then-delete nke nwere oghere oge n’etiti ha abụọ bụ otú otu job Build na-arụ ọrụ siri tufuo
  `_work` ya na 2026-08-27. Janitor na-eme check na removal n’otu nzọụkwụ;
  hapụ ya ka o mee ya.
- Ịkwụsị runner n’etiti job na-akagbu job ahụ (a hụrụ nke a ozugbo): jiri `systemctl stop` naanị
  mgbe listener ya enweghị child `Runner.Worker` — ma mee ya n’otu command.
- Workflow agaghị echekwa artefact na `/tmp` (ọ bụ RAM). Budata ha na
  `$RUNNER_TEMP` (dị na disk, nke runner ọ bụla nwere nke ya) — artefact `next-build` nke 1.3 GB were nkeji 27–32
  iji rute na tmpfs, ma were nkeji 2 iji si na disk bulite ya.
- VPS `.15` bụ naanị maka homologation — ọ naghị arụ CI runner ma ọlị.

## Ụzọ ndị a na-elekọta (runner GitHub na-elekọta)

Akaụntụ ahụ nọ na atụmatụ **Pro**: **ọrụ 40 ndị a na-elekọta nwere ike ịgba n'otu oge**, nke nlele PR ọ bụla,
usoro ọrụ ọ bụla push na-akpalite, na nhazi ndabara CodeQL na-ekekọrịta. Ntụle e mere na 2026-10-18 n'oge
mkpọsa merge (~merge 10 kwa elekere na `release/v3.8.52`) gosiri: **e mepụtara ọsọ usoro ọrụ 2,500 n'ime
24 h**, push 44 gaa na branch ahụ n'ime 6 h, **ọrụ 106 ndị a na-elekọta nọ n'ahịrị nchere** mgbe nlele PR chere ruo
ọtụtụ awa ma e mere merge PR na-enweghị ha. Usoro ọrụ itoolu malitere na push ọ bụla gaa na branch ahụ:

| kwa push gaa na `release/v*`                | ọrụ ndị a na-elekọta | ihe mere n'okpuru oke mkpọtụ ahụ                               | kemgbe 2026-10-18                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------------------: | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `ci.yml` (matrix zuru ezu)                  |               ruo 30 | push sochirinụ kagburu ya, 24 n'ime 24 n'otu ụbọchị            | anaghị amalite na push `release/**` (ọsọ PR + Release-Green)                              |
| `quality.yml`                               |                   ~6 | kagburu, 23 n'ime 23                                           | anaghị amalite na push `release/**`                                                       |
| `Release-Green` `--quick`                   |                    0 | gbara na `omni-build`, a kagburu 97 n'ime 100                  | oghere `omni-release`, anaghị akagbu ya ma ọlị, ọsọ na-echere na-ejikọta                  |
| `Lock released branch`, `OpenSSF Scorecard` |                    2 | otu ọsọ nọ n'ahịrị nchere maka push ọ bụla                     | otu ọsọ kwa ref (`tests/unit/build/hosted-lane-concurrency.test.ts`)                      |
| `Release acceptance` (akụkọ shadow)         |                    1 | otu `npm ci` a na-elekọta kwa push, anaghị akagbu ya ma ọlị    | agbanwebeghị — o kwesịrị ka e jiri nzacha `paths:`                                        |

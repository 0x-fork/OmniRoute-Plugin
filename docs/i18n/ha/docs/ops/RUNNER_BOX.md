Pool ɗin da ake host da kansa yana aiki a kan na’urar **.113** (`tomni-proxmox-113`). Tun daga 2026-10-18, kowane
runner na OmniRoute yana da **role guda ɗaya**, label guda ɗaya, da kasafin memory/CPU da aka daidaita da abin da workload ɗinsa
ya nuna a awo. Slot na Turbopack yana samun abin da build ɗin ke buƙata; sauran slots an daidaita su da
mafi girman amfani da aka auna. Na’urar tana yin overcommit ne kawai idan build na Turbopack ya zo daidai da build na webpack na Release-Green
da kuma aikin lint; swap na host (16 GB) yana ɗaukar wannan nauyin, kuma saboda Builds na pull-request suna gudana
a hosted runners, slot na Turbopack baya aiki a mafi yawan lokaci:

| runner               | label          | role                                                                                                                                                           | `MemoryHigh` / `MemoryMax` | `CPUQuota`    |
| -------------------- | -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- | ------------- |
| `omniroute-113-5`    | `omni-build`   | Builds na Turbopack: `ci.yml` `Build` (push/dispatch kawai), Docker amd64, `npm-publish`, `nightly-v4-build`, `preview-artifact`, build na a11y na kowane dare | 56 GB / 60 GB (swap 0)     | 1600 %        |
| `omniroute-113-6`    | `omni-release` | Release-Green (`release-green` + `main-green`), build na webpack                                                                                               | 20 GB / 24 GB (swap 4 GB)  | 800 %         |
| `omniroute-113-2`    | `omni-light`   | ayyukan dare-dare (Schemathesis, promptfoo, garak, k6, fara a11y) da `quality.yml` `lint-guard`                                                                | 12 GB / 16 GB (swap 4 GB)  | 600 %         |
| `omniroute-113`      | `omni-light`   | pool iri ɗaya da na sama                                                                                                                                       | 8 GB / 12 GB               | 400 %         |
| OmniHeuris, OmniMind | (repos ɗinsu)  | Dependabot kawai (mafi girman amfani 155 MB kowannensu, 2026-10-18)                                                                                            | 4 GB / 6 GB                | 200 % kowanne |

An auna a 2026-10-18:

| --------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| RAM / CPU | **78 GB / 32 cores**         | build na production na Turbopack ya kai kololuwar 49 GB ba tare da iyaka ba (`tests/unit/ci-build-bundler-webpack.test.ts`); a Next 16.3.8, compile phase kaɗai yana riƙe da ~40 GB (36 GB RSS a cikin `next-build` tare da loader workers 17 masu 3 GB, an auna 2026-10-18); build na webpack yana amfani da ~14–16 GB. Limits ɗin suna cikin `/etc/systemd/system/actions.runner.<…>.service.d/override.conf` na kowane unit
(an adana tsoffin values a matsayin `override.conf.bak-20261018`) kuma an yi amfani da su kai tsaye ta
`systemctl set-property --runtime … MemoryHigh=… MemoryMax=… CPUQuota=…` — ba tare da restart ba, don haka babu
wani job da ke gudana da aka taɓa. Ana canza labels ta runners API
(`gh api -X DELETE repos/<repo>/actions/runners/<id>/labels/<label>` /
`gh api -X POST repos/<repo>/actions/runners/<id>/labels -f 'labels[]=<label>'`), shi ma kai tsaye.

## Sanya mai tsaftacewa (sau ɗaya kawai, a kan sabar)

```bash
scp scripts/ops/runner-janitor.sh root@192.168.0.113:/opt/omniroute-ops/runner-janitor.sh
ssh root@192.168.0.113 'chmod +x /opt/omniroute-ops/runner-janitor.sh; apt-get install -y lsof'
# cron (root): kowane minti 30, a rubuta log zuwa /var/log/runner-janitor.log
*/30 * * * * MAX_ACTIVE_RUNNERS=6 /opt/omniroute-ops/runner-janitor.sh >> /var/log/runner-janitor.log 2>&1
```

Ana buƙatar `lsof`: mai tsaftacewar yana tabbatar da cewa babu aiki a wata hanya ta hanyar ɗaukar hoton fayilolin da suke buɗe sau ɗaya kafin ya cire ta, kuma idan babu wannan kayan aikin ba ya cire komai, sannan ya faɗi hakan
(exit 1). Da farko, gwada kowane canji da `--dry-run` — yana buga ainihin abin da zai
yi ba tare da taɓa komai ba.

Abin da yake yi a kowane gudu: yana share ragowar namu (`runner-*`, `omniroute-*`,
`next-build*`, `e2e-build.tar.gz`) bayan **sa'a 3 a kan tmpfs** da sa'a 24 a kan faifan
`_work/_temp`; yana kashe `next-build` da ya wuce minti 75 (babu wani job da ke gudana har tsawon haka — a
2026-08-27, wani ya gudana na minti 70 bayan GitHub ya ayyana job ɗinsa a matsayin wanda ya ɓace); yana cire
checkout da suka kai sa'a 48 na runner waɗanda unit ɗinsu yake **tsayawa**; yana aika faɗakarwa idan faifai ya kai ≥ 85 %, memory PSI
`full/avg60` ya kai ≥ 10 %, da kuma idan masu sauraro sun fi `MAX_ACTIVE_RUNNERS` yawa (tare da
rarrabuwar omniroute/wasu). Exit 1 = ana buƙatar kulawa; karanta log ɗin.

Dokar minti 75 ba ta gano build wanda GitHub ya riga ya hakura da job ɗinsa ba. Lokacin da
`Runner.Listener` ya kasa sabunta job ɗinsa, `_diag/Runner_*.log` yana nuna
`POST request to …/renewjob timed out after 100 seconds`, API kuma yana nuna job ɗin a matsayin `completed`
(failure) bayan kusan minti goma daga sabuntawa na ƙarshe da ya yi nasara, sannan `next-build` ya ci gaba da gudana
ba tare da job da ke haɗe da shi ba (2026-10-18: an nuna Build na #17066 a matsayin wanda ya gaza da ƙarfe 19:43, amma build ɗinsa har yanzu
yana riƙe da 40 GB minti biyar bayan haka). Tabbatar da ref a cikin sabon `_diag/Worker_*.log`, sannan
ka aika SIGTERM ga bishiyar build kawai ta PID — `next-build`, `npm run build`, `build-next-isolated.mjs` da
ma'aikatan `_work`. Kada ka taɓa `Runner.Listener`, `RunnerService.js` ko `Runner.Worker`: worker ɗin
yana bayar da rahoton cewa an soke job ɗin, sannan listener ɗin ya koma online da kansa.

## Unit na runner: KillMode

Tsohon saitin `KillMode=process` na runner yana barin `Runner.Worker → npm → next-build`
suna gudana idan an tsayar ko aka sake kunna wani unit — build marar uwa yana ci gaba da cin RAM da
CPU ba tare da job da ke haɗe da shi ba. Kowane unit na OmniRoute yana da drop-in
(`/etc/systemd/system/actions.runner.diegosouzapw-OmniRoute.<name>.service.d/10-killmode.conf`)
mai ɗauke da `KillMode=mixed`: SIGTERM ga listener da farko, sannan SIGKILL ga dukan cgroup a
`TimeoutStop`. Zai fara aiki a sake kunna unit ɗin nan gaba — sake kunna **runner ɗaya a
kowane lokaci, kuma sai lokacin da ba ya aiki**, tare da binciken rashin aiki da sake kunnawar a cikin umarni ɗaya.

## Dokokin gudanarwa

- **Ginin Turbopack guda ɗaya kacal a lokaci guda — ana tilasta hakan ta hanyar label.** Duk aikin da ke gudanar da cikakken
  `next build` na Turbopack yana nufin `[self-hosted, omni-build]`, kuma **`omniroute-113-5`** ne kaɗai yake ɗauke da wannan
  label ɗin: `ci.yml` `Build`, `npm-publish.yml` `publish`, `nightly-v4-build`, `preview-artifact`,
  ginin a11y na dare (#16853) da kuma **amd64** na `docker-publish.yml` (webpack a cikin BuildKit —
  Turbopack ya rushe a can, gudu na 33253576569). Reshen Docker na arm64 yana ci gaba da kasancewa a `ubuntu-24.04-arm`.
  amd64 na `docker-publish` yana amfani da rukunin concurrency na `heavy-build-main` tare da `ci.yml` `Build`
  (`cancel-in-progress: false`). Dole ne Docker Engine ya kasance a kan `omniroute-113-5`. Gurbin Turbopack
  na biyu ba zai shiga cikin 78 GB ba (2 × 33–37 GB tare da sauran abubuwan da ke kan na’urar); gurbi na biyu yana da aminci ne kawai ga
  gine-ginen webpack.
- **Gine-ginen pull-request suna gudana a kan hosted (2026-10-18).** `ci.yml` `Build` yana isa `omni-build` ne kawai don
  `push` da `workflow_dispatch`; kowane `pull_request` (na ma’ajiyar asali ko fork) da `merge_group` Build
  yana gudana a kan `ubuntu-latest` tare da webpack. Lokacin da aka tura kowane PR na ma’ajiyar asali zuwa gurbin, wani Build na Turbopack
  ya riƙe shi na mintuna 29–58, Builds guda 18 suka yi layi har zuwa awa 18, kuma push Build na layin fitarwar da kansa
  ya samu gurbin sau ɗaya kacal cikin ayyuka 40. A kan hosted, irin wannan PR Build yana ɗaukar mintuna 8–12 tare da sauran
  ayyukan PR ɗin. Turbopack, bundler ɗin da `npm-publish` ke fitarwa, yana ci gaba da samun gwaji a layin fitarwa ta hanyar
  ginin a11y na dare na default branch (04:41 UTC), ta kowane push ko dispatch Build da
  ya isa gurbin, da kuma ta `npm-publish` kansa. `tests/unit/build/heavy-build-queue.test.ts`
  yana tabbatar da ƙofar mai sharuɗɗa huɗu.
- **Kada a taɓa rage girman gurbin Turbopack ƙasa da buƙatarsa da aka auna.** A 2026-10-18 da ƙarfe 18:06 an
  rage gurbin daga 56G/60G zuwa 40G/44G domin samar wa `omni-release` sarari. Matakin compile ya tsaya a
  `MemoryHigh` tsawon duk gudunsa (memory PSI `full` 30–43 %), kuma `Runner.Listener`, wanda
  yake amfani da cgroup ɗin tare da shi, bai iya sabunta aikinsa ba. An maido da 56G/60G na mai shi daga 2026-10-17 a
  wannan rana. Idan aka sa ƙasa da wannan ƙimar, ginin ba ya gaza; yana ta fama da swapping, sannan aikin ya ɓace.
- **Release-Green yana da nasa gurbin (2026-10-18).** Kafin wannan, dukkan tantancewar Release-Green suna gudana
  a kan `omni-build`: a kowane push (~10/awa, an soke 97 cikin gudu 100 na push a tsakiyar aiki cikin rana ɗaya)
  da cikakken sweep guda uku na kusan awa 2 a rana (sweep na 05:29 ya jira gurbin na awa 6) — an ɗora wa gurbin gini
  guda ɗaya ayyuka fiye da ƙarfinsa sau da yawa, kuma PR Builds na ma’ajiyar asali sun jira na tsawon awanni.
  `release-green` da `main-green` yanzu suna nufin `omni-release` (`omniroute-113-6` kaɗai) tare da
  `OMNIROUTE_USE_TURBOPACK=0`, kuma sabon push ba ya ƙara soke gudun da ya riga ya fara (amma
  gurbin da ke jiran aiki har yanzu yana haɗe guguwar ayyukan zuwa guda ɗaya). `tests/unit/build/runner-capacity-routing.test.ts`
  yana tabbatar da routing, sauyawa zuwa webpack, da kuma cewa babu wani abu a kan `omni-release` da yake yin gini da Turbopack.
- **Rukunin ayyuka masu sauƙi: `omni-light`.** `omniroute-113` da `omniroute-113-2` suna gudanar da ayyukan dare da ke buƙatar
  `next build` na backend kawai (~5–6 GB; webpack) da `quality.yml` `lint-guard`. `omniroute-113`
  yana da `MemoryHigh=4G` har zuwa 2026-10-18 — ginin promptfoo ya shafe mintuna 54 yana ƙoƙarin kwato memory
  a can; 8 GB yana kawar da throttling.
- **An rage yawan listeners da gangan.** An kashe units huɗu na OmniRoute a 2026-08-29 — kowane ƙarin
  listener wani mai amfani ne na daban ga kasafin memory da ke sama. Iyakar janitor ita ce 6 (`MAX_ACTIVE_RUNNERS=6` a cikin cron): yana ƙirga
  kowane `Runner.Listener` da ke kan na’urar, kuma OmniHeuris + OmniMind suna ƙara guda biyu zuwa namu guda huɗu.
- **Kada a taɓa tsaftace `/tmp` ko `_work` da hannu yayin da wani runner ke aiki.** Yin
  dubawa-sannan-sharewa tare da tazara tsakanin matakan biyun shi ne yadda wani Build job mai gudana ya rasa
  `_work` ɗinsa a 2026-08-27. Janitor yana yin dubawar da cirewar a mataki guda;
  a bar shi ya yi.
- Tsayar da runner a tsakiyar aiki yana soke aikin (an gani kai tsaye): a yi `systemctl stop` ne kawai
  lokacin da listener ɗinsa ba shi da ɗan `Runner.Worker` — kuma a yi hakan cikin command guda ɗaya.
- Kada workflows su ajiye artefacts a cikin `/tmp` (RAM ne). A sauke zuwa
  `$RUNNER_TEMP` (a kan disk, na kowane runner) — artefact na `next-build` mai girman 1.3 GB ya ɗauki mintuna 27–32
  kafin ya sauka a kan tmpfs, amma ya ɗauki mintuna 2 kawai kafin a upload daga disk.
- VPS na `.15` na homologation kawai ne — ba ya taɓa gudanar da CI runners.

## Layin da aka masauƙa (masu gudanarwa da GitHub ke masauƙa)

Asusun yana kan tsarin **Pro**: **ayyuka 40 da aka masauƙa masu gudana lokaci guda**, waɗanda kowane binciken PR,
kowane workflow da push ya kunna da kuma saitin tsoho na CodeQL suke amfani da su tare. An auna a 2026-10-18 yayin
kamfen ɗin merge (~merge 10/awa a kan `release/v3.8.52`): **an ƙirƙiri gudanarwar workflow 2,500 cikin
awa 24**, push 44 zuwa branch ɗin cikin awa 6, **ayyukan da aka masauƙa 106 sun yi layin jira** yayin da binciken PR ya jira na
tsawon awanni kuma aka yi merge na PR ba tare da su ba. Workflow tara ne suka kunna a kowane push zuwa branch ɗin:

| a kowane push zuwa `release/v*`             | ayyukan da aka masauƙa | sakamako yayin cunkoson                                                    | tun daga 2026-10-18                                                               |
| ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------: | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `ci.yml` (cikakken matrix)                  |            har zuwa 30 | push na gaba ya soke shi, 24 cikin 24 a rana                               | ba a fara shi a kan push na `release/**` ba (gudanarwar PR + Release-Green)       |
| `quality.yml`                               |                     ~6 | an soke, 23 cikin 23                                                       | ba a fara shi a kan push na `release/**` ba                                       |
| `Release-Green` `--quick`                   |                      0 | ya gudana a kan `omni-build`, an soke 97 cikin 100                         | gurbin `omni-release`, ba a taɓa sokewa ba, gudanarwar da ke jira tana haɗewa     |
| `Lock released branch`, `OpenSSF Scorecard` |                      2 | gudanarwa ɗaya a layin jira ga kowane push                                 | gudanarwa ɗaya ga kowane ref (`tests/unit/build/hosted-lane-concurrency.test.ts`) |
| `Release acceptance` (rahoton inuwa)        |                      1 | `npm ci` guda ɗaya da aka masauƙa ga kowane push, ba a taɓa sokewa ba      | babu canji — ya dace a yi masa matatar `paths:`                                   |
| `Publish to Docker Hub` → `Deploy to VPS`   |                      2 | ana soke shi a kowane push; ɓangaren amd64 yana gogayya a kan `omni-build` | babu canji — tashar deploy ta mai shi                                             |
| Saitin tsoho na CodeQL (`dynamic`)          |                      4 | `javascript-typescript`, `actions`, `c-cpp`, `python` ga kowane push       | babu canji — Settings → Code security; `c-cpp`/`python` ba sa duba komai a nan    |

`tests/unit/build/push-storm-diet.test.ts` yana tabbatar da trigger na push na `ci.yml` da
`quality.yml` wanda aka keɓe ga `main` kawai. Illar gefe: Codecov ba ya ƙara karɓar loda na tushe ga kowane push na release (matakin
na bayani ne kuma yana da `continue-on-error`); ƙofar coverage da ke cikin repo da sharhin PR
ba su shafa ba.

**Ƙara ƙarfin self-hosted ba zai gyara layin jiran da aka masauƙa ba.** Devbox ɗin
(`devbox-vm-06-dev002`, 24 vCPU / 54 GB) zai iya masaukin runner, amma zaman agent ɗin sun riga
sun riƙe load ɗinsa a 20–26, container ɗin agent ba shi da Docker daemon, kuma layin da ya cika
shi ne wanda aka masauƙa. A wannan lokacin na’urar `.113` tana da 71 GB a sarari kuma load 5–8; abin da ke takura mata
shi ne kasafin ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya ga kowane listener da ke sama (iyaka listener 6), ba CPU ba. Sauran hanyoyin
gyaran sun shafi tsari ne: a shigar da PR na kamfen ta jerin gwanon Mergify (`label=queue`, batches suna amfani tare da
gudanarwar CI ta `merge_group` guda ɗaya maimakon gudanarwa ɗaya ga kowane PR da kuma ɗaya ga kowane push), a rage harsunan
saitin tsoho na CodeQL, sannan a tace `release-acceptance.yml` bisa fayilolin da yake karantawa.
